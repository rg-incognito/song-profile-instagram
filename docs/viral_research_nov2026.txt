1. VISUAL ART STYLE — WHAT'S WORKING
======================================

Current style (flat couple illustration + deep colour background) is well-positioned.

TRENDING ILLUSTRATION STYLES:
- Lofi anime / 2D flat illustration — dominant aesthetic for music reels
  Cozy scenes: warm lamp, rain, couples together
  Illustration sits BEHIND the music, not competing with it
  Little motion — almost static with just a slow zoom

- Film grain on flat 2D — rising add-on in 2025
  Clean flat illustration + subtle grain texture = depth without complexity
  Easy to add in ffmpeg, makes reels look hand-crafted

- Deep jewel tones — perform better on dark Reels interface than pastels
  Maroon, navy, forest green, burnt sienna — already doing this right
  Pastels wash out when displayed full-screen

- Earthy muted palettes — reaction to oversaturated feeds
  Signals "sophisticated, not trying too hard"
  Good for indie/quiet mood content

WHAT TO TRY NEXT:
- Add very subtle grain texture pass to generated images (ffmpeg)
- Try cooler jewel tones: slate blue, deep plum alongside warm palette
- Blur glow layer: zoom-blurred duplicate behind sharp image = depth halo
  Very common on editing tutorial channels right now


2. HINDI INDIE SONGS — WHAT'S TRENDING
========================================

ESTABLISHED BUT REEL-CORE ARTISTS:

Aditya Rikhari
  Songs: Sahiba, Paro, Raat Bhar, Raatein Guzaari, Rehle Mere Kol, Faasle
  Style: mellow, vocal-driven, emotional

Anuv Jain
  Songs: Afsos (ft AP Dhillon), Inaam, Jo Tum Mere Ho, Arz Kiya Hai (Coke Studio)
  Style: soft indie pop, deeply romantic

Prateek Kuhad
  Songs: Pyar Ki Si, Just Like A Movie, Blush, Cold Mess, Kasoor
  Style: folk-indie, conversational Hinglish

UNDERRATED / LESSER KNOWN — ADD TO PIPELINE:
- Salman Elahi — quieter, less known, romantic, deserves more reach
- Justh — reviewers say "deserves more love from audience"
- Kushagra — "Finding Her" is known but deeper catalogue is underused
- Ritviz — warm, festival-adjacent, works for dreamy summer scenes

WHAT MAKES A HINDI SONG WORK FOR REELS:
- Slow tempo (not necessarily slow BPM, but unhurried feel)
- Conversational Hindi/Hinglish lyrics — sounds like talking to someone
- The kind of lyric line you'd screenshot and send to someone
- Avoid heavy bass drops — they break visual stillness
- 1:30-2:30 mark is usually where the best hook is in indie songs


3. ENGLISH SONGS — QUIET / ATMOSPHERIC ANGLE
==============================================

Underexplored niche for this style of content.
Target genres: bedroom pop, dream pop, indie folk, indie R&B, lo-fi singer-songwriter

ARTISTS THAT FIT (non-mainstream, aesthetic-reel worthy):

Wasia Project (London sibling duo — Olivia Hardy + William Gao)
  Minimal piano, jazz touches, cinematic strings
  Perfect for slow zooms, very few Indian creators use them

Nilüfer Yanya
  Laid-back guitar, emotional depth, indie-cool
  Performed with Adele and Lorde — credible but not overplayed

Jodie Nicholson
  Album: Safe Hands
  Described as "intimate, soothing, achingly vulnerable"

Mariae Cassandra
  Diaristic songwriting, hazy off-kilter production
  Late night feel, very few listeners = less saturated

Leo Walrus
  Warm, nostalgic, dreamy — almost no one using this for reels

Men I Trust
  Dream pop / synth — extremely popular in aesthetic communities
  "Organon" and "Show Me How" work well visually

Iron & Wine (Sam Beam)
  2026 album Hen's Teeth (Sub Pop)
  Song "In Your Ocean" — lyric-visual storytelling, tropical warmth
  Quote from artist: "I prefer to tell it in pictures rather than explain feelings"

Clairo
  Still growing, perfectly suited to couple illustration scenes
  "Bags", "Amoeba", "Pretty Girl" type tracks

beabadoobee
  Bedroom pop, bittersweet — works with hand-drawn aesthetic

KEY RULE FOR ENGLISH SONGS:
  Pick lines that FEEL translated — emotional even if viewer doesn't speak English
  Example type: "I keep your photograph / and she don't know"
  Avoid very American-culture-specific references


4. EDITING STYLE — WHAT'S ACTUALLY VIRAL
==========================================

CONFIRMED WORKING:
  Smooth eased zoom (not linear/robotic) — keyframe the curve
  Blur glow layer: zoom-blurred duplicate behind sharp image (very common now)
  White glow or neon glow on bold Hindi font — opacity ~80% so it blends
  Text position: upper frame, never on the face
  Original/lesser-known audio — trending audio gets discovered but also diluted

LENGTH:
  15-30 seconds wins for completion rate
  30s is sweet spot for lyrics
  "A 30s reel with high completion beats a 3min reel that loses viewers halfway"

HOOK (first 3 seconds):
  Bold lyric line OR the most emotional moment of the song
  Don't save the best for the end — open with it

CAPTIONS:
  3-5 hashtags max (not 20)
  Include keywords in caption TEXT itself (Instagram reads captions for discovery)
  2200 char limit — use it to tell a micro story, not just dump hashtags

POSTING:
  3-5 reels per week consistently beats 7 in one day then nothing
  "Early morning" timing (less competition at the first scroll of the day)

EMERGING TREND — CINEMATIC MINIMALISM:
  The "overcrowded effects" era is fading
  Clean image + one motion + one lyric line = higher-performing aesthetic
  Our current style is ahead of this curve
